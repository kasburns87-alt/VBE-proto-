# HVAC Revenue Recovery — Design Directions

## Three Possible Directions

### 1. Signal Furnace

**Very Brief Intro:** A premium industrial-editorial system inspired by field dispatch boards, service manuals, and high-performance operations rooms. It feels decisive, quietly technical, and built for owners who value control.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 2. Modern Field Journal

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, photographic service-business journal with sun-faded materials, practical storytelling, and human-first credibility. It emphasizes the craft of running a dependable local operation.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 3. Precision Ledger

**Very Brief Intro:** An elegant finance-adjacent interface centered on metrics, proof, and conversion visibility. It uses generous whitespace and typographic precision to make the offer feel analytical and high trust.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Chosen Direction — Signal Furnace

### Design Movement

**Industrial editorial design**: a contemporary interpretation of field-service dispatch boards, operations manuals, bold print layouts, and technical annotation. The visual language must make the offer feel like disciplined commercial infrastructure—not a chatbot, generic agency, or software template.

### Core Principles

1. **Commercial clarity over feature density.** Every section converts an operational problem into an outcome, signal, or next move.
2. **Asymmetry with discipline.** Large editorial type, offset sections, and hard rules create momentum without visual clutter.
3. **Operational texture.** Fine grids, tick marks, thin rules, condensed labels, and evidence-like microcopy suggest an intelligently run control system.
4. **Human-controlled automation.** The system feels accountable and calm; it highlights ownership, clear handoffs, and business visibility.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **warm ivory**, communicating clarity and openness for a public-facing service brand. Dense **charcoal** creates authority in key story moments, while **furnace orange** is used sparingly for action, sequence, and urgency. Steel-gray supports technical annotation without introducing cold corporate blue. The orange should read as an operational signal, not decoration.

### Layout Paradigm

The site uses an **editorial field report** structure rather than a centered SaaS landing page. Hero content is offset against a visual operational diagram. Content sections alternate between wide editorial statements, slim evidence rails, process sequences, and staggered grids. The reading path deliberately moves left-to-right between issue, system, proof, and action.

### Signature Elements

1. A **signal rail** of orange tick marks and short labels that appears across key sections.
2. **Diagonal furnace marks**—subtle orange slash motifs that convey handoff, momentum, and conversion flow.
3. **Operations annotations**: small uppercase labels, ratio-like numerals, and thin technical rules surrounding major claims.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like controlled mechanical feedback. Buttons compress very slightly when pressed, text links reveal an orange rule, and navigation remains direct. There is no novelty animation; every interaction reinforces accountability and forward movement.

### Animation

Use only short transform-and-opacity entrances, gated by reduced-motion preferences. Text and process steps should enter in a 40–70 ms stagger, while the hero visual uses one calm, low-distance fade-up. Hover states use underlines or minor x-axis movement, never glow effects or long transitions.

### Typography System

Use **Barlow Condensed** for compact labels and operational numerals, **DM Sans** for readable interfaces and navigation, and **DM Serif Display** for selected editorial subheadings. Headline hierarchy uses heavy italic DM Sans or a tight display treatment; body copy stays open, calm, and legible. Never use Inter.

### Brand Essence

**HVAC Revenue Recovery installs the operating layer that turns paid inquiries into visible, owned, booked work for independent HVAC teams.**

Personality: **decisive, accountable, commercially sharp.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and outcome-led. CTAs invite a practical diagnostic rather than a generic demo. Microcopy should be specific, calm, and respectful of a busy owner’s time.

Examples:

> “Every inquiry needs an owner.”

> “Map the handoff. Recover the work.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a compact **offset signal bracket**: two thick, staggered orange strokes that imply an inbound lead being captured and routed forward. The wordmark uses a condensed all-caps treatment with a small orange tracking mark between “Revenue” and “Recovery.”

### Signature Brand Color

**Furnace Orange — `#E85D32`**. It is reserved for decisions, handoffs, and calls to action.
